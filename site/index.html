03 / LOOK INSIDE

What drives a player's number?

Choose a player to see the calculation.

Effect is this player's change in expected strokes per round from each weighted field. Negative is better. Each tournament score is drawn from a normal distribution with this expected round score × 4 and standard deviation equal to round variation × 2, with the selected player’s variation multiplier applied. A lower score wins. This is mathematically equivalent to adding four independent normally distributed rounds.

THE BIG PICTURE

A simulation doesn't predict the future. It shows what would happen across many possible versions of the tournament under a set of assumptions.

Know the limits. The model doesn't include cuts, playoffs, weather, injuries, or late field changes. The original forecast used 2022–2026 score data available before the event. Strokes-gained, traditional, form and course-fit stats come from PGA Tour's unofficial stats feed and are not fitted to results. The weights are your assumptions, not tested predictors. Experiment results are local to your browser and are not saved forecasts or betting advice.
