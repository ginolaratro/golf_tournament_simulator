03 / LOOK INSIDE

What drives a player's number?

Choose a player to see the calculation.

Each tournament score is drawn from a normal distribution with this expected round score × 4 and standard deviation equal to round variation × 2. A lower score wins. This is mathematically equivalent to adding four independent normally distributed rounds.

THE BIG PICTURE

A simulation doesn't predict the future. It shows what would happen across many possible versions of the tournament under a set of assumptions.
